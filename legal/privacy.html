MailShell Privacy Policy

Last updated: {{BUILD_DATE}}.

Summary

MailShell is a free desktop app that shows Gmail in its own window. It does not collect, sell or share your personal information. It has no accounts with its author, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Everything the app keeps is stored on your own computer.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the MailShell app only. Your Gmail messages, contacts and Google account are handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. Websites you open from MailShell are governed by their own policies.

What MailShell stores on your computer

  • Gmail session: cookies and cached data that keep you signed in to Gmail, kept in the MailShell app data folder. Signing out, or clearing data in Settings, removes them.
  • Preferences: your choices in Settings (theme and whether the Gmail display changes are on), in a preferences file in the app data folder.
  • Downloads: attachments you download are saved to your Downloads folder, where they stay until you delete them.

Network connections

MailShell connects only to:

  • Google services needed to show Gmail (for example mail.google.com, accounts.google.com, and Google's attachment and image servers), and
  • websites you choose to open. These open in your default web browser, except Google pages, which open in MailShell.

The attachment list for an open conversation is requested from Google using your existing Gmail session. Nothing is sent to the author.

What the author receives

Nothing. MailShell does not send usage data, identifiers, message content or error reports to its author or any third party.

Passwords

MailShell does not ask for or store your Google password. You sign in to Google inside the MailShell window, exactly as you would in a browser.

Security

No software is perfectly secure. You are responsible for your computer, your Google account settings and your own backups. Keep Windows and MailShell up to date.

Children

MailShell is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app. The date at the top is the date this copy of MailShell was built.
