MailShell Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026.

Summary

MailShell is a free desktop app that shows Gmail or Exchange / Microsoft 365 in a window of its own. It doesn't collect, sell or share your personal information. There is no MailShell account, and no analytics, advertising or crash reporting. Everything the app keeps is stored on your own computer.

Who this policy covers

This policy covers the MailShell app only. Your Gmail messages, contacts and Google account are handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy. Your Exchange or Microsoft 365 messages, contacts and Microsoft account are handled by Microsoft under the Microsoft Privacy Statement. Websites you open from MailShell are governed by their own policies.

What MailShell stores on your computer

  • Mail session: the cookies and cached data that keep you signed in to Gmail or Exchange, stored in MailShell's app data folder. Clear Cache in Settings removes cached data but keeps you signed in. Uninstalling MailShell doesn't remove this data; delete the app data folder to remove it. The folder is %APPDATA%\MailShell on Windows, ~/Library/Application Support/MailShell on macOS, and ~/.config/MailShell on Linux.
  • Preferences: your choices in Settings: your theme, your mail service, and whether Gmail UI presets is on. They're stored in a preferences file in the app data folder.
  • Downloads: attachments you download are saved to your Downloads folder, where they stay until you delete them.

Network connections

MailShell connects only to:

  • Google or Microsoft services needed to show your mail (for example mail.google.com, accounts.google.com, outlook.cloud.microsoft, the Microsoft sign-in pages, and Google's attachment and image servers), and
  • websites you choose to open. These open in your default browser, except the Google and Microsoft pages that MailShell needs for mail and sign-in, which open inside MailShell so you can sign in.

For Gmail, the attachment list for an open conversation is requested from Google using your existing Gmail session. Nothing is sent to the author.

What the author receives

Nothing. MailShell does not send usage data, identifiers, message content or error reports to its author or any third party.

Passwords

MailShell never asks for or stores your Google or Microsoft password. You sign in on Google's or Microsoft's own sign-in page inside the MailShell window, just as you would in a browser. Work and school accounts may sign in through your organization's own sign-in page.

Security

No software is perfectly secure. You are responsible for your computer, your Google or Microsoft account settings and your own backups. Keep your operating system and MailShell up to date.

Children

MailShell is not directed at children under 13, and it does not collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version ships with the next release of MailShell and is posted on this website. The date at the top shows when this version was published.
