Gmail in its own window. Free, and open source.

MailShell is a desktop app for Gmail on Windows. It keeps Gmail in a window of its own, with the attachments and unread count you need at a glance.

Download for Windows View the source

Early beta, version 0.1.0. Windows 10 or 11. Free under the MIT License.

Built for reading mail

MailShell does the parts of Gmail you use all day, and keeps out of the way for the rest.

Every attachment, in one bar

MailShell lists every file in the open conversation above the messages, so you never hunt through a thread for one. Click a PDF or photo to open it in your browser. Other files save straight to your Downloads folder.

Unread mail, at a glance

Your unread count sits on the MailShell button in the Windows taskbar. See what's waiting without bringing Gmail to the front. Past nine, it shows 9+.

Know who wrote, without switching

New mail that arrives while you're in another app pops up with the sender and subject. Click it and MailShell comes straight back to the front. You can switch these off in Windows notification settings.

Stays running, out of the way

Close the window and MailShell carries on in the system tray, so new mail still reaches you. Quit from the tray menu whenever you want it gone.

Your theme, Gmail included

Pick light, dark or system in Settings, and Gmail follows the same choice. The whole app matches, even when Windows is set to something else.

A calmer, tighter Gmail

MailShell trims the clutter: a shorter header, no Upgrade, Support or Studio buttons, a slimmer sidebar, and two-line mail rows with clear dividers. What's left is your mail. Prefer stock Gmail? Turn the Gmail UI presets off in Settings.

Private by design

MailShell is a window onto Gmail. It has no accounts of its own, no analytics, and no ads.

Your data stays on your computerYour sign-in is kept in the app's data folder. Signing out or clearing data removes it.
No trackingMailShell sends nothing to its author. Gmail talks only to Google, as it does in a browser.

Platforms

MailShell is an early beta, so expect rough edges. Windows is the most tested platform. The Mac and Linux builds are new and may not be stable.

WindowsInstaller. The most tested platform.
MacUnsigned build. The first time you open it, right-click the app and choose Open. May not be stable.
LinuxAppImage. May not be stable on every distribution.

Found a problem? See open issues or report a new one. Mac and Linux reports are especially welcome.
